\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

Parenting is one of the most profound journeys a human being can undertake. 
It asks us, repeatedly and without mercy, to show up fully---not just as 
caregivers and providers, but as mirrors, mentors, and companions to the 
small people who look to us to help them make sense of the world.

Yet for many parents, that calling can feel overwhelming. We love our 
children deeply, and still---in the heat of a tantrum, in the exhaustion of 
a sleepless night, or in the middle of an argument with a teenager---we find 
ourselves reacting in ways that don't reflect the parent we want to be.

This book is written for those moments.

\textit{The Empathetic Parent} is not a book of perfect answers. It is a 
practical guide grounded in decades of research in developmental psychology, 
neuroscience, and family systems therapy. Its central premise is simple: 
\textbf{children who feel understood behave better, learn faster, and grow 
into more resilient adults}---and parents who practice empathy experience 
more joy, less conflict, and deeper connection.

The pages that follow are organized as a journey: from understanding what 
empathy is and why it matters, to the everyday skills of active listening 
and emotion coaching, to navigating the particular challenges of each 
developmental stage. Along the way you will find reflection questions to 
invite honest self-examination, practice exercises to turn insight into 
habit, and key insights that distill the most important ideas.

No matter where you are in your parenting journey---whether you are 
expecting your first child, raising a strong-willed toddler, navigating the 
turbulent waters of adolescence, or simply hoping to repair and deepen a 
relationship with a grown child---there is something here for you.

You do not need to be a perfect parent. You only need to be a 
\textit{present} one.
